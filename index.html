01 About

Hi! I'm a Data Scientist and AI Engineer. For the last 2+ years I've been building GenAI systems end to end: retrieval over private knowledge, agents that call real tools, and the Python services that put both in front of people.

Most of my work is the unglamorous half of GenAI: how a document gets chunked, why a retrieval returns the wrong passage, what a prompt should refuse to answer. I build on LangChain, LangGraph and CrewAI, with Pinecone, ChromaDB and FAISS behind them.

Alongside that sits classical ML with Scikit-learn, PyTorch and XGBoost. Everything ships the same way: FastAPI, Docker, Jenkins CI/CD, PyTest and AWS.
